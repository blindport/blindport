The middle ground

Managed simplicity, local privacy

Preview tunnels are useful for short-lived sharing. Managed cloud gateways make ongoing services easy, but commonly decrypt web traffic. A private VPS keeps control local, but adds another server to operate. Blindport offers a durable address without either tradeoff.
